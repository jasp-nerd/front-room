# Logos

Pip's head beside "Front Room" in Gabarito ExtraBold, set as outlines (logo/source/build_wordmark.py). The head carries the character; the word stays plain so it reads at any size and in one colour.

- `wordmark.svg`: navy letters (#14202f) on white, mist and the colour fields. Minimum width 96px.
- `wordmark-reversed.svg`: white letters, for the navy field and the video.
- The lockup is Pip's head (Pip group, `pip-head.webp`) then the word, as the `Wordmark` component draws it with `variant="lockup"`.
- `app-icon-512.png` and `favicon-48.png` (Icons group): Pip's head on a sun tile, built by logo/source/build_icons.py.

The logo is on the TV only on the join screen; during play the small whistle on the lower third signs our card instead.

Clear space is the height of Pip's head on every side. Never recolour Pip, outline or stretch the lockup, set "Front Room" in another face, or place it beside a score.
