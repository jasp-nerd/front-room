# Cast

The family on the demo sofa, drawn: Rob, Ana, Leo and Sam. Each fan has a colour, and their helper (a small robin, their personal agent) wears a scarf in it.

| Fan | Colour | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Rob | sky | the stats lover |
| Ana | sun | wants it simple, in Spanish |
| Leo | pitch | ten years old |
| Sam | pink | would rather listen than look |

## Components

- `FanFace` (`id`, `size`): the head-and-shoulders portrait.
- `Helper` (`id`, `size`): that fan's helper robin.
- `castColour`: each fan's colour token name (`--sky`, `--sun`, `--pitch`, `--pink`).

## Rules

- The helpers are not Pip. Pip wears the whistle and decides; the helpers wear scarves and ask.
- On the shared TV, never show which helper asked: the TV shows how many, not who.
